Stroke Numerals Font Generator

This page will generate a StrokeNumerals.ttf file based on your stroke-numeral design. The mapping is:

  • 0–9 → chopped (square) numerals
  • ) ! @ # $ % ^ & * ( → stretched numerals (Shift+0–9 on a US keyboard)

After installing the font, type 0123456789 for chopped digits and )!@#$%^&*() for stretched digits in any app that lets you choose fonts.
